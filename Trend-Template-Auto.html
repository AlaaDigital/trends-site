iPhone 17 Pro Max Leak: Titanium Design & A19 Chip - What We Know So Far

Apple's next flagship just leaked in full CAD renders. Lighter titanium frame, A19 Pro chip with 30% GPU boost, and a 10x periscope zoom are coming this September.

𝕏🔗↗

1. The Design - Finally Thinner

According to CAD files shared by reliable leaker Majin Bu, the iPhone 17 Pro Max will drop weight from 221g to 198g thanks to a new grade-5 titanium alloy. Bezels are down to 1.15mm.

2. A19 Pro - The Biggest Jump Since A15

Benchmarks leaked on Geekbench 6 show single-core 3,450 and multi-core 10,200 - a 25% jump over A18 Pro. The secret is TSMC 2nm.

“This is the first time Apple will use 2nm for iPhone. Efficiency gains are insane - 35% less power.” — Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo

3. Camera - 10x Periscope Finally

After years behind Samsung, Apple is adding a 48MP tetraprism with 10x optical zoom and 100x digital with AI stabilization.

  • Main: 48MP f/1.6 with 8K video
  • Ultra: 48MP with macro
  • Periscope: 48MP, 10x optical, 100x AI

What It Means For You

If you own iPhone 14 or older, this is the year to upgrade. If you have 15 Pro Max, wait for reviews on battery - 2nm should add 2 extra hours.

Price leak: $1,199 base 256GB (same as last year).
